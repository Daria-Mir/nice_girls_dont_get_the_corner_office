About the Book & Mindmap

Why 'Nice Girls' Hold Themselves Back

In *Nice Girls Don't Get the Corner Office*, executive coach Dr. Lois P. Frankel reveals that women are not failing to succeed due to lack of talent or ambition. Rather, they are held back by unconscious, self-defeating behaviors learned in childhood.

Socialized to be "nice"—polite, quiet, accommodating, and nurturing—women carry these rules into the workplace, where they translate into career barriers. This interactive workbook breaks down 133 common mistakes categorized into 7 core behavior domains.

"A nice girl is a woman who behaves as if she is still a child, conforming to socialization rules that no longer apply in adulthood."

About the Author

Dr. Lois P. Frankel, PhD

Executive Coach & Bestselling Author

Dr. Lois Frankel is the President of Corporate Coaching International, a consulting firm based in Pasadena, California. She is an internationally recognized expert in leadership development for women, literally writing the book on coaching women to succeed in businesses large and small.

She is a licensed psychotherapist and coach whose books, including Nice Girls Don't Get the Corner Office, Nice Girls Don't Speak Out, and Nice Girls Just Don't Get It, have been translated into over twenty-five languages. Her practical coaching guidelines show women how to eliminate unconscious, self-defeating behaviors learned in childhood. Read more about the author.
